Population genomics

Breed ancestry pipeline and canine panel design

Client: Darwin's Ark

Darwin's Ark needed a maintainable breed ancestry pipeline for low-pass dog and cat genomes and better coverage from its custom canine sequencing panel.

What I delivered

  • Refactored a shell-script pipeline into a modular Nextflow pipeline for breed ancestry from low-pass WGS of dog and cat genomes.
  • Identified low-coverage regions to directly improve custom probe design for a canine genomics panel.

Rare disease

De novo trio assembly to confirm a pathogenic variant

Client: Gaudi Bio

Gaudi Bio needed to confirm the pathogenic variant in a rare disease patient before designing a custom therapy.

What I delivered

  • Performed de novo assembly of PacBio HiFi whole-genome data from a rare disease proband and both parents to confirm the pathogenic variant.
  • Delivered results that informed the downstream design of a custom therapy.

How a project runs

Clear scope up front, regular check-ins, and deliverables you own.

  1. Scope

    A free call to understand your question, data, and timeline. You get a written proposal with a fixed quote.

  2. Analyze

    Work happens in version-controlled code with progress updates at agreed milestones.

  3. Review

    We walk through results together and refine the analysis based on what you learn.

  4. Hand off

    You receive results, figures, code, and documentation, ready to reuse or extend.
