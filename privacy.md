This page explains what information is collected when you visit embedbio.github.io, the website of Embed Bioinformatics LLC.

## What we collect

**We don't ask you for personal information on this site.** There are no forms, accounts, or newsletter sign-ups. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to discuss your project, and we don't share them.

**Advertising cookies.** This site uses the Google tag (gtag.js) from Google Ads. It sets cookies that help us measure whether our ads bring people to the site. Google may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, and the ad you clicked to get here. Google's use of this information is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google explains [how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your choices

- Turn off personalized ads at [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.
- Browsers that send a Global Privacy Control signal can be set to ask sites not to sell or share your data.

We don't sell personal information.

Questions about this policy: [aindap@embedbioinformatics.com](mailto:aindap@embedbioinformatics.com)
