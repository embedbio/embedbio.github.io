As a former PTA President and current Treasurer, I spend a lot of time on paperwork, mostly checking reimbursement forms and matching cash deposits to the bank. To speed this up, and to learn LangGraph along the way, I built [pta-treasurer-agent](https://github.com/indapa/pta-treasurer-agent).

It includes two agents:

- **Reimbursement Agent:** checks that the receipts submitted with a reimbursement form add up to the amount claimed.
- **Cash Verification Agent:** checks that the amount on a cash verification form matches the bank deposit slip.

I still review every result, since scanned or handwritten documents can be misread. Even so, the tools make my volunteer work faster, and building them gave me hands-on experience with AI agents.
